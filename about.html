About Pegasus Depot

The ventilation specialist with the stock to prove it.

Pegasus Depot is an independent European retailer of premium vehicle ventilation, roof hatches and interior lighting. We source straight from the OEM production line, hold real stock in our own Dutch warehouse and ship across Europe within 24 hours.

Our story

Built for bodybuilders. Now open to everyone.

The ventilators, valves, hatches and lights in this shop have been fitted for years by bus builders, ambulance converters, horsebox manufacturers and van outfitters across Europe, usually through trade channels only. Pegasus Depot opens that same OEM-grade catalogue to every workshop, fleet and owner who wants to order online, at a fair price, from stock.

  • OEM-grade qualityEvery product comes from the same production line the vehicle builders rely on.
  • Real stock, real speedThousands of units on the shelf. Order before 15:00 CET and it ships the same working day.
  • Specialists, not call centresQuestions about cut-outs, airflow or voltage are answered by people who know the products inside out.
