FAQ

Questions about campers.

Will a roof fan drain my leisure battery?

The Winglet draws 3 W. The Round and Le Mans draw 80 W at full speed, but with the Speedcontrol you typically run them at 10 to 20 W overnight.

Can I still use my roof rack or solar panels?

The Turbo III (59.5 mm) and exhaust ventilator are designed to sit under roof racks. The Le Mans is only 64 mm high and sits happily next to solar panels; check the drawing for exact dimensions.
