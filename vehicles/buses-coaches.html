FAQ

Questions from buses & coaches owners.

Are the roof hatches homologated?

Yes. The glass dome is homologated ECE 43R, the hatches EC 2004/104, and the 970 x 530 mm versions comply with EC 2001/85 as emergency exits.

Do the electric hatches work on 12V?

They are 24V units. For 12V vehicles we supply the 89-STEP12-24 converter.
