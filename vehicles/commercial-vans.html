The van that stays fresh sells itself.

FAQ

Questions from commercial vans owners.

Electric or wind-driven?

Wind-driven (Turbo II, Turbo III, exhaust ventilator) is silent, free to run and maintenance-free, but only works while driving. Electric (Le Mans, Round, Winglet) ventilates on demand, also when parked.
