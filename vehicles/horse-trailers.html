FAQ

Questions about horse trailers.

12V or 24V?

Horse trailers pulled by a car or van: 12V. Horse trucks built on a truck chassis: usually 24V. When in doubt, check the vehicle's battery system.

Can I close the vent in winter?

Yes, with the V12 or Flower Power interior valve. The snow filter keeps snow and insects out of the intake.
