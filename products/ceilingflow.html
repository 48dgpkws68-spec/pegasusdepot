The professional ceiling fan that drops into a standard 600 x 600 suspended-ceiling tile. 130 m³/min, 27 to 41 W, 35 to 47 dB(A), remote control included. Cools in summer, destratifies heat in winter. Black or white.

Ceilingflow is the plug-and-play ceiling fan for offices, workshops, showrooms, shops, schools, kitchens, meeting rooms and server rooms. Remove a ceiling tile, insert the Ceilingflow (595 x 595 x 166 mm, 5.5 kg), plug it in and play. No installation costs, no installer, under five minutes.

With 130 m³/min of airflow at only 27 to 41 W and a noise level of 35 to 47 dB(A), it cools on warm days and pushes warm air back down on cold days, making optimal use of the vertical temperature profile of your heating. Three speeds, reversible direction and timer on the RF remote. Brushless motor with short-circuit and peak-voltage protection, recyclable ABS, RoHS, CE and LVD certified, IP44.

Remote control included (can also be operated without). Frame for solid ceilings available separately. One unit covers roughly 40 to 50 m².
