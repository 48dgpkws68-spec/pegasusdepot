A rotating, wind-powered extractor that keeps pulling stale air out of your van, dog transporter or horse trailer without a single wire. Around 245 m³/h at motorway speed. Ø 319 mm cowl, 128 mm cut-out, only 68 mm high so it fits under most roof racks.

The Turbo II is the classic mushroom-style rotating vent, refined. Airflow over the roof spins the UV-stabilised ABS cowl on two ball bearings and a venturi effect pulls air out of the vehicle: roughly 245 m³/h at 100 km/h. No wiring, no power draw, no noise. It simply works whenever the vehicle moves or the wind blows.

At 68 mm high it fits under most roof racks. The 128 mm bore hole and 26 mm installation depth make it an easy retrofit, and the kit includes an interior grille and closable shutter so you can shut it off in winter.

Perfect for transporting dogs, horses and cargo that must not sit in stale air. Combine it with a floor ventilator for a continuous flow even when parked.
