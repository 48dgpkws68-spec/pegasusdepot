The compact 80 mm floor ventilator. Same double function as the 129 mm model (gas removal and roof-fan boost) in a smaller footprint for small vans, dog boxes and compartments.

The 80 mm floor ventilator brings the same logic to small vehicles: create a low inlet so your Winglet, Turbo III or exhaust ventilator can actually move air, and let heavy gases escape at floor level.

PC/ABS, black RAL 9005, Ø 80 mm cut-out, Ø 119 mm outer, 90 mm deep, 0.4 kg. Recommended with any mechanical or electric rooftop ventilator.
