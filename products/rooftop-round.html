The same 850 m³/h motor as the Le Mans in a round ABS housing, only 62 mm high. Blow and suck with the 3-way switch, reduce noise with a speed controller. Loved in campers, caravans, trailers, houseboats and loading areas.

Our round electric rooftop ventilator delivers a healthy supply and exhaust of air in commercial vehicles, campers and cars. It shares the Le Mans motor platform (850 m³/h, 80 W) but uses a round Ø 320 mm cover, just 62 mm high, that suits many classic vehicle roofs and fits under most roof racks.

Use the 3-position switch to choose intake or extraction, and add the speed controller to dial the airflow (and the noise) down when you only need a trickle of fresh air overnight.

Available in 12V and 24V, white or black, and as a motorless version that can be motorised later. The cover can be painted in your body colour.

Le Mans or Round: which should I pick?

Technically identical (850 m³/h, 80 W). The Le Mans has a lower, more aerodynamic cover; the Round suits classic roofs and tight spaces. Both use a 230 mm opening.
