The long-life version of the Le Mans. A brushless motor pulls 515 to 565 m³/h of air out of your vehicle at only 34 W, with no brushes to wear out. Ideal for damp loading areas, animal transport, chemical transport and coaches.

The Le Mans LL takes the proven Le Mans shape and fits it with a brushless motor. The result: a ventilator that runs for years without servicing, draws only 34 W and is available with an integrated speed control unit.

The LL is an extraction-only ventilator, which is exactly what most loading areas, animal compartments and coach toilets need: a constant, healthy discharge of humid or contaminated air. Pair it with a floor ventilator for a complete airflow loop.

Order the 12V or 24V version, with or without built-in speed control. A 3-way switch and interior valve are available separately. Saw template included.

What is the difference with the standard Le Mans?

The LL uses a brushless motor (longer life, lower power draw) and extracts only. The standard Le Mans can blow and suck and moves more air (850 m³/h).

Can I control the speed?

Yes. Choose the version with integrated speed control unit, or add our separate Speedcontrol 30-3004.
