The 24V control panel for our electric roof hatches. Opens and closes the front and rear of one or two hatches, shows dome position on green LEDs, auto-closes when the ignition goes off, and still allows ventilation when parked.

Always use the 30-6000 with our electric roof hatches. This compact 24V control unit manages one or two hatches: open or close the front and rear side independently, and read the dome position on the green LED display.

Safety and comfort are built in. The unit closes the hatches automatically when the ignition key is turned off, but the hatch can still be opened for ventilation while parked. The display has a current-save function and the push buttons are illuminated with orange LEDs for operation in the dark.

EMC approved and homologated according to EC 2004/104. For 12V vehicles a 12V to 24V converter is available on request.
